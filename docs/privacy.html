Privacy policy

Shri Tools, an app by Shri Labs · last updated 27 October 2026

Shri Tools is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no server, no accounts and no analytics, and Shri Labs does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What stays on your computer

Your stories, characters, pictures, videos, settings and logs are saved only in the Shri Tools folder on your own disk. You can delete them at any time by deleting that folder.

YouTube (optional)

Shri Tools uses the YouTube API Services. If you connect a YouTube channel, you sign in with Google and allow Shri Tools to manage your YouTube account (permission youtube.force-ssl). Shri Tools uses this access only to do what you ask it to:

  • read your channel's name and ID, so you can see which channel is connected;
  • upload the videos and Shorts you choose, with their title, description, tags, thumbnail and subtitles;
  • create or add to playlists for your seasons, if you switch that on.

The sign-in key Google gives Shri Tools is stored only on your computer. It is sent only to Google, never to Shri Labs or anyone else. Shri Tools does not read, change or delete anything else on your channel and does not sell, share or transfer your data.

Shri Tools' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

  • In Shri Tools: open YouTube and press Disconnect. Shri Tools deletes the stored key and tells Google to cancel it.
  • At any time from Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Shri Tools → Remove access.

Videos you already uploaded stay on YouTube until you delete them there.

Other optional services

If you choose a cloud story AI (for example Gemini, Claude or OpenAI) or connect a Telegram bot in the settings, Shri Tools sends the text needed for that feature (your story idea, or a status message) directly from your computer to that service with your own key. Those services handle that text under their own privacy policies. Without these settings nothing is sent.

Children

Shri Tools is a tool for video makers and is not meant for use by children. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
