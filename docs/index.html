Shri Tools

Shri Tools is a free desktop app for making animated stories. You write a story idea, and Shri Tools draws every scene, makes the characters act and speak, checks every clip and hands you the finished episode with Shorts, titles, a description, subtitles and thumbnails. It runs on your own Windows computer.

Get it on GitHub Privacy policy

YouTube uploads

If you choose to, you can connect your own YouTube channel to Shri Tools. It then uploads the videos you made, with their Shorts, thumbnails, subtitles and playlists, to that channel: right away or on a schedule you set. Shri Tools only uploads and manages the videos you tell it to. You can disconnect a channel at any time.

Good to know

  • Everything runs on your computer. Your stories, pictures and videos stay on your disk.
  • Shri Tools has no account system and no server of its own.
  • The source code is open on GitHub.
